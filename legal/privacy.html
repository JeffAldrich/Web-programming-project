PRIVACY POLICY

At JAC – Timeless & Limitless, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website and how that information may be used.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you create an account, place an order, contact us, or use certain features of our website, we may collect information such as your name, email address, contact information, and account details.

We may also collect information about products you add to your cart or wishlist.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

The information we collect may be used to:

  • Create and manage your JAC account
  • Process and manage orders
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain your cart and wishlist
  • Send newsletter updates when you subscribe
  • Improve our website and services

3. ACCOUNT INFORMATION

If you create a JAC account, you are responsible for providing accurate information. Your account information should be kept secure and should not be shared with other people.

4. NEWSLETTER

If you subscribe to the JAC newsletter, we may use your email address to send information about new collections, promotions, announcements, and other JAC updates.

You may choose to stop receiving newsletter communications at any time.

5. PROTECTING YOUR INFORMATION

We take reasonable steps to protect the information provided through our website. However, no website or online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. SHARING YOUR INFORMATION

JAC does not sell or rent your personal information. Information may only be shared when necessary to provide requested services, operate the website, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

7. COOKIES AND BROWSER STORAGE

Our website may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to remember certain information and improve your browsing and shopping experience.

8. YOUR INFORMATION

If you have an account with JAC, you may contact us regarding the information associated with your account and request corrections where applicable.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

JAC may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

10. CONTACT US

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, you may contact JAC using the contact information provided on our website.
